A LITTLE ABOUT NOVA

Search, without the detour.

Nova keeps the search experience on this page. Search results are fetched by the site's Cloudflare Worker and displayed here. Opening a result takes you directly to that result in a new tab, so you can keep Nova open.

Search queries are sent to DuckDuckGo to retrieve results. Nova is an independent interface and is not affiliated with DuckDuckGo.
